About us

DAKER is a local steel shelving factory founded in 2012 in Phnom Penh. We started as a small workshop with five staff and today employ a team of more than sixty.

We handle every stage in house — cutting, bending, welding and powder coating. That is why we can guarantee the quality and still price below imported shelving.

Our customers range from small neighbourhood shops to supermarkets, distribution warehouses, garment factories, schools and offices across all 25 provinces.

What we stand for

Quality before price

We do not thin the steel to hit a lower price. A shelf must carry the load we print on it.

Honest pricing

The price on the site is the price you pay. No hidden charges.

After-sales support

Any structural fault inside the warranty period is repaired or replaced free.

Local jobs

Our workforce is 100% Cambodian, and we train young people in welding and fabrication.
