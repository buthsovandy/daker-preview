DAKER · Technology

Every steel sheet is cut by CNC

We do not cut steel by hand. Each shelf drawing goes into the computer and the CNC machine cuts to that drawing, so holes, edges and sizes come out identical every time — and custom sizes are quick to make.

  • Cut from a computer drawing Sizes and hole positions follow the drawing, not a steady hand.
  • Identical every time Ten sheets or a thousand — parts always fit together.
  • Custom sizes Need a non-standard size? We change the drawing and cut it right away.
  • Clean edges, then coating Cut edges are smooth and safe to handle, then welded and powder coated.

04 · Process

How we manufacture

From raw steel to a shelf ready to install

  1. 01

    Quality raw steel

    We select sheet steel at the right gauge for strength and long service life.

  2. 02

    Design & engineering

    3D drawings and load calculations are finished before production starts.

  3. 03

    CNC cutting

    CNC machines cut every part to exact dimensions, with no drift between batches.

  4. 04

    Welding & fabrication

    Skilled welders join the structure so it stays square and rigid under load.

  5. 05

    Coating & inspection

    Powder coating protects against rust, then every unit is checked before it ships.

  6. 06

    Delivery & installation

    Carefully packed, delivered to your site, and installed by our own team.

07 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what customers ask us most

Do you deliver to the provinces? What does it cost? +

We deliver to all 25 provinces. Phnom Penh is free; provinces follow our delivery rate table. Orders over $1,000 ship free nationwide.

How long is the warranty? +

Five years on the structure, plus after-sales repair service.

Can I assemble it myself? +

Gondola and boltless shelving assemble in 15–45 minutes with no special tools. Heavy-duty pallet racking is installed on site by our crew.

How can I pay? +

Cash on delivery, bank transfer via ABA or Wing, or instalments for orders over $500.

Can I get a size or colour not in the list? +

Yes. We manufacture in-house, so we can design to your space. Send us your dimensions or a photo on Telegram and we will draw it up and quote.

Do you do site surveys for large projects? +

Yes. For supermarkets, warehouses or multi-branch stores we survey and design on site free of charge.

Where are the shelves made? Can we visit the factory? +

They are made in our own factory in Stung Meanchey, Phnom Penh — we are not a reseller of imported shelving. You are welcome to visit: call ahead and we will show you the cutting, bending, welding and coating stages, and the shelves being built.
